What the course covers

Six modules from the institute outline. The 3-month batches cover the foundation. The 6-month batches continue through diagnostics and programming.

  1. 1

    Electrical fundamentals and safety

    Meters, basic circuits, and workshop safety.

  2. 2

    Battery, starting, and charging

    Batteries, alternators, and starter circuits.

  3. 3

    Wiring and relay systems

    Harness layout, central wiring, and relays.

  4. 4

    Sensors, actuators, and EFI

    Engine sensors, injectors, and the EFI system.

  5. 5

    Diagnostics and scanners

    OBD-II connection and computerized diagnostics.

  6. 6

    CAN bus and module programming

    ECU, body modules, and key-fob programming.
